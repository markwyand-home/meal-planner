Roasted Broccoli and Tofu Bowls with Peanut Sauce

TofuVegetarianserves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 block (12-16 oz) extra-firm or super firm tofu
  • 2 TBSP soy sauce (marinade)
  • 1 TBSP rice vinegar (marinade)
  • 1 TBSP sesame oil (optional) (marinade)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (marinade)
  • 1 TBSP fresh ginger, grated (or 1 tsp dry ginger powder)
  • 1 TBSP arrowroot powder or cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup natural peanut butter (sauce)
  • 2 TBSP soy sauce (sauce)
  • 1 TBSP rice vinegar (sauce)
  • 1 TBSP maple syrup (sauce)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (sauce)
  • 2 tsp sesame oil (optional) (sauce)
  • 1 1/2 cups brown or white rice
  • 2 bunches broccoli (about 1 lb) (even frozen works)
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 large orange sweet potato, cubed
  • 1 cup carrots, zucchini, or other vegetables (optional)
  • garnish: chopped peanuts, cilantro, sesame seeds, green onions, chili garlic sauce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Start by marinating the tofu. Press the tofu if desired, then cut into small cubes. Stir the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger together in a medium bowl. Toss the tofu in the marinade gently and let sit for at least 10 minutes (up to several days in the fridge).

  2. While the tofu sits, start cooking the rice: bring a pot of water to a boil, add the rice, and boil (about 30 minutes for brown rice, about 10 for white), then drain. Pour it back in the pot, cover, and keep warm.

  3. Preheat the oven to 400F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Arrange the broccoli, bell pepper, sweet potato, and any other vegetables on the sheet. Spray lightly with olive oil if desired, and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Bake 25-30 minutes, tossing halfway through, until golden brown and tender.

  4. After the tofu has marinated at least 10 minutes, pour it onto a separate baking sheet in an even layer and bake alongside the vegetables for 25-30 minutes, tossing halfway, until golden brown.

  5. Meanwhile, make the peanut sauce by stirring all the sauce ingredients together in a small jar or measuring cup (or blend together), adding hot water a tablespoon at a time if it needs thinning.

  6. To serve, arrange bowls of rice, vegetables, and tofu. Drizzle peanut sauce liberally over everything, and top with cilantro, crushed peanuts, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and/or chili garlic sauce.

Notes: Tofu can marinate 10 minutes up to several days. Frozen broccoli works without thawing. Doubling the peanut sauce is recommended.
