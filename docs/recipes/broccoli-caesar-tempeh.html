Broccoli Caesar with Smoky Tempeh Bits

TempehVegetarianserves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp (30 mL) raw cashew butter
  • 2 Tbsp (30 mL) filtered water
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp (22 mL) fresh lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated with a microplane grater
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) minced capers
  • 1 Tbsp (15 mL) nutritional yeast
  • 3 Tbsp (45 mL) extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 bunch broccoli, cut into florets
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) pure maple syrup
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp (2 mL) gluten-free tamari soy sauce
  • 1 tsp (10 mL) extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 block (4 oz/114 g) tempeh, crumbled
  • 2 tsp (10 mL) nutritional yeast (garnish)
  • Freshly ground black pepper (garnish)

Instructions

  1. To make the Creamy Cashew Caesar Dressing: in a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine cashew butter, water, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Stir until the cashew butter is broken up, mashing chunks against the sides of the jar to integrate it. Add garlic, Dijon mustard, capers, nutritional yeast, and olive oil. Tightly secure the lid and shake vigorously until the dressing is smooth and creamy. Set aside.

  2. To make the salad: bring a large saucepan of water to a boil over medium-high heat. Add a fat pinch of salt and the broccoli florets, and simmer until the broccoli is just tender and bright green, about 4 minutes. Drain and run under cold water to stop the cooking. Set aside.

  3. In a small bowl, stir together the paprika, smoked paprika, maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, and tamari. Set aside.

  4. Dry the saucepan and return it to the stove over medium heat. Add the oil and let it heat until shimmering slightly. Add the crumbled tempeh, spreading it into a single layer. Let it sit and brown for a full 2 minutes, then stir and let sit for another minute. Pour the paprika mixture into the pan — it should sizzle. Stir to coat all of the tempeh, then remove from heat.

  5. Place the broccoli on a serving platter. Drizzle the Creamy Cashew Caesar Dressing overtop. Scatter the smoky tempeh bits over as well. Garnish with nutritional yeast and freshly ground black pepper. Serve immediately.

Notes: Broccoli can be blanched (as written) or roasted at 400 F for ~20 min for a warm salad. Pairs well as a side or light main; add grain to bulk up.
