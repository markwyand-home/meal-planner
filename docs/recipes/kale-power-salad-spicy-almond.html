Kale Power Salad with Spicy Almond Dressing

TofuVegetarian55 minserves 4

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sliced almonds, soaked for 1 hour and drained (dressing)
  • 2 cloves garlic, peeled (dressing)
  • 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast (dressing)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (dressing)
  • 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar (dressing)
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice (dressing)
  • 1 teaspoon Tamari (dressing)
  • 1 teaspoon maple syrup (dressing)
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard (dressing)
  • 1-2 teaspoons hot sauce of choice (dressing)
  • 2 teaspoons nutritional yeast (salad)
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sweet paprika
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 500 grams), peeled and diced
  • 1 350 gram block firm OR extra firm tofu, drained and torn
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided (salad)
  • 2 tablespoons arrowroot powder
  • 2 tablespoons cornmeal
  • 6 cups chopped kale, lightly packed
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice (salad)
  • 1 teaspoon maple syrup (salad)
  • 1 ripe avocado, peeled, pitted and chopped
  • 1 small red onion, sliced thin
  • 1/4 cup toasted sliced almonds

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.

  2. Make the spicy almond dressing: in a blender, combine the almonds, garlic, nutritional yeast, water, olive oil, white wine vinegar, lime juice, tamari, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, hot sauce, salt, and pepper. Blend until smooth and creamy, adding a splash more water if too thick. Taste and adjust seasoning; set aside.

  3. Make a spice blend: stir together the nutritional yeast, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and chili powder. Put the sweet potatoes in one bowl and the tofu in another; blot the tofu very dry with a clean towel first.

  4. Divide the spice blend between the sweet potatoes and tofu. Drizzle both with olive oil (1½ tbsp each) and season with salt and pepper. Add the arrowroot and cornmeal to the tofu only, then toss each until evenly coated.

  5. Spread the sweet potatoes and tofu on opposite sides of the baking sheet, giving each piece space. Roast 35 minutes, stirring/flipping halfway, until the sweet potatoes are tender and the tofu is crunchy outside.

  6. Combine the kale with the remaining tablespoon of olive oil, lime juice, maple syrup, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Massage with your hands until slightly tenderized, about 1 minute.

  7. Drizzle the kale with some of the dressing, then top with the roasted sweet potatoes, tofu, avocado, and red onion. Drizzle with more dressing and finish with the toasted sliced almonds. Serve immediately.

Notes: Serves 4-6. Sunflower seeds or cashews can sub for almonds. Dressing is great doubled for grain bowls. Requires a blender.
