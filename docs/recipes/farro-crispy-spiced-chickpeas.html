Farro with Crispy Spiced Chickpeas, Tomatoes and Leeks

ChickpeasVegetarian65 minserves 5

Ingredients

  • 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided, plus more for drizzling
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter
  • 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 2 leeks, white and light green parts, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced into half moons
  • 2 cups diced fennel
  • Fresh thyme sprigs
  • 10 ounces pearled or semi-pearled farro
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro, plus more for garnish
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley, plus more for garnish
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated or minced
  • 1 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest and 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • Flaky sea salt, for serving

Instructions

  1. Using a clean kitchen towel or paper towels, dry the drained chickpeas thoroughly. In a large skillet, heat 3 tablespoons of the oil over medium-high heat. Add the chickpeas, 1 teaspoon of the salt, and a grind or two of black pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp, 10 to 12 minutes. Add the butter, fennel seeds, and red pepper flakes and cook until golden and nutty smelling, 1 to 2 minutes longer. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in 2 tablespoons of the lemon juice. Transfer the chickpeas to a plate and season with salt to taste. Wipe out the skillet.

  2. Return the skillet to medium-high heat and add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil. Add the leeks and fennel and a pinch each of salt and black pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and browned, about 7 minutes.

  3. Add 3 cups water, the thyme, and the remaining 1 1/2 teaspoons salt and bring to a simmer, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Stir in the farro. When the liquid returns to a simmer, lower the heat to medium-low and cover the pan. Cook until the farro is tender, 25 to 30 minutes (semi-pearled farro cooks more quickly than pearled), stirring occasionally. If the pan dries out, add a little more water.

  4. Discard the thyme sprigs. Stir in the tomatoes, parsley, cilantro, garlic, lemon zest, and a little more lemon juice. Taste and add more salt and lemon juice if needed.

  5. Just before serving, stir in the crispy chickpeas and more parsley and cilantro, drizzle with a little more oil, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
