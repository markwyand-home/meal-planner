Lemony Baked Rice with Artichokes, White Beans, and Caramelized Leeks

BeansVegetarian70 minserves 4

Ingredients

  • 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 leeks, white and light green parts, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced into half moons
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 1/2 cups basmati rice, rinsed and drained
  • 1 1/2 cups marinated artichokes, drained and chopped
  • 1 (15.5-ounce) can white beans (such as cannellini or Great Northern), drained and rinsed
  • 2 1/2 cups boiling vegetable stock or water
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced or chopped fresh basil, chives, mint, or fennel fronds, plus more for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.

  2. In a shallow 2-quart casserole or gratin dish, or an 11 x 7-inch baking dish, combine the leeks, oil, 1/2 teaspoon of the salt, the red pepper flakes, and a grind or two of black pepper. Grate the zest from the lemon into the baking dish and toss well. Cut the zested lemon in half and set aside. Spread the leek mixture in an even layer and roast until it starts to caramelize, about 25 minutes.

  3. Sprinkle the rice evenly over the leeks, then top with the artichokes, beans, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt. Add the boiling stock, then seal the pan tightly with foil. Bake until the rice is tender, 20 to 22 minutes.

  4. Remove from the oven and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff the rice with a fork. Squeeze the juice from one of the reserved lemon halves over the rice, then stir in the Parmesan and herbs. Season to taste with salt and black pepper. Serve with the remaining lemon half cut into wedges, and more Parmesan and herbs, if you like.

Notes: From the cookbook Dinner in One (photographed page, approx. p.156). Times and nutrition are estimated (page gives oven times only: 25 min roast + 20-22 min bake + 5 min rest). Book's swap tip: if you can't find good marinated artichokes, use sun-dried or oil-packed tomatoes instead.
