French Onion Baked Lentils and Farro

LentilsVegetarian105 minserves 6

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds (910 grams) yellow onions (4 large or 5 medium), sliced
  • 2 tablespoons (30 grams) unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon (15 grams) olive oil
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons (30 ml) dry sherry, vermouth, or white wine (optional)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups (300 grams) dried farro
  • 3/4 cup (135 grams) dried lentils, green (le puy) or brown (italian)
  • A few sprigs of fresh thyme
  • 6 cups (1.4 liters) vegetable broth + splashes more, if needed
  • 2 cups (8 ounces or 225 grams) coarsely grated gruyere cheese

Instructions

  1. Caramelize the onions: heat butter and olive oil in a large, deep saute pan (ovenproof if you have one) over medium heat. Add the onions, toss to coat, and cover. Reduce heat to medium-low and let steep 15-20 minutes. Uncover, raise heat to medium-high, stir in about 2 tsp kosher salt, and cook, stirring every few minutes, until brown, soft, and sweet, about 20 minutes more, reducing heat to medium once their liquid cooks off.

  2. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

  3. Assemble the casserole: add garlic to the onions and cook, stirring, 1 minute. Add the sherry (if using) and cook until it disappears. Add the farro and cook 2 minutes. Add the lentils, 6 cups broth, and thyme, and bring to a simmer. Season well with salt and pepper. If your pan isn't ovenproof, transfer to a casserole dish.

  4. Bake, covered, stirring about halfway through, until the lentils and farro are tender, 40-50 minutes. If the liquid absorbs before then, add another 1/4-1/2 cup broth — aim for a risotto-like looseness. Fish out the thyme stems.

  5. Sprinkle with gruyere and broil until browned on top.

Notes: Serves 6 to 8. Gluten-free option: sub shortgrain brown rice for farro. Keeps 4-5 days in fridge; base can be made ahead and cheese broiled before serving.
