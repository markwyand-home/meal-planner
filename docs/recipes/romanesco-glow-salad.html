Meyer Lemon Romanesco Glow Salad

Nuts & SeedsVegetarian20 minserves 4

Ingredients

  • 1/4 teaspoon Meyer lemon zest (dressing)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh Meyer lemon juice (dressing)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard (dressing)
  • 2 tablespoons grapeseed or other neutral-flavoured oil (dressing)
  • 1 medium head Romanesco broccoli (or cauliflower), broken up into florets
  • 1 medium sweet apple, cored and chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, small dice
  • 1 cup seedless grapes, halved
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 teaspoons minced fresh sage (about 1 sprig)
  • 1/3 cup walnut halves, toasted and chopped

Instructions

  1. Make the dressing: in a small bowl, whisk together the Meyer lemon zest, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, grapeseed oil, salt, and pepper until combined. Set aside.

  2. Make the salad: working in batches, pulse the Romanesco florets in a food processor until couscous-like in size and texture. Transfer to a large bowl.

  3. Add the apple, celery, grapes, green onions, sage, and walnuts to the bowl. Season with salt and pepper and toss to combine.

  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss. Serve immediately, or store in the fridge up to 3 days.
