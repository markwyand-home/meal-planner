Better Than Takeout Dan Dan Noodles

MeatContains meat30 minserves 6

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup + 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or smashed
  • 1-2 tablespoons crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup low sodium soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup hoisin sauce (make sure to use GF, if needed)
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons creamy peanut butter or tahini
  • 8 ounces Chinese style egg noodles, or rice noodles
  • 2 1/2 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 pound ground chicken or pork
  • 2 cups mixed mushrooms, chopped
  • black pepper
  • 1 medium shallot, chopped
  • 4 green onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Make the chili oil: heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add 1/3 cup of the sesame oil, the garlic, and chili flakes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the garlic is fragrant, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and carefully transfer the oil to a heatproof bowl or jar.

  2. Meanwhile, combine the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, honey, vinegar, peanut butter (or tahini), and 1/3 cup water in a bowl.

  3. Cook the noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside. To the same pot, add the broth and half of the soy sauce mixture. Bring to a simmer over medium heat and stir in the spinach. Keep warm.

  4. Place the skillet used for the chili oil over medium-high heat. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons sesame oil and the chicken (or pork). Season with black pepper and brown all over, breaking it up as it cooks, about 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms and shallot and cook another 2-3 minutes. Slowly pour in the remaining soy sauce mixture, bring to a simmer, and cook until the sauce coats the meat and it begins to caramelize and crisp, about 5 minutes. Stir in 2-4 tablespoons of the chili oil. Remove from heat.

  5. Ladle the broth into bowls, add the noodles, and toss to combine. Spoon the meat over the noodles and top with green onions and additional chili oil.

Notes: Vegetarian: double mushrooms and use veg broth. Hoisin subs: soy sauce, miso paste. Keeps 2-3 days in fridge. Reviewers report longer than 30 min.
